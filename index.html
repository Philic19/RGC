The work

What does a Map Labeler do?

You interpret satellite imagery and convert visible features into structured digital map data.

🏢

Building Creation

Draw building footprints on imagery and create accurate shapes.

  • Trace building footprints
  • Follow corners and curves
  • Separate structures correctly
🛠️

Building Repair

Correct existing building shapes and fix inaccurate labels.

  • Fix shifted polygons
  • Split or merge shapes
  • Remove false positives
🛣️

Transportation

Create road centerlines and apply the required measurements.

  • Follow road curves
  • Keep lines centered
  • Follow project instructions
🌿

Landcover

Classify land areas based on their visible characteristics.

  • Forest
  • Water
  • Urban and agricultural areas

Frequently asked questions

Common questions

Here are answers to the questions new applicants commonly have.

Do I need previous experience?
Training materials and exams are provided for the mapping workflow. You still need to learn the project rules and complete the required training tasks.
Is this work from home?
The work is performed using a computer and the required software. Follow the current project instructions for the exact working arrangement and requirements.
How much can I earn?
Earnings depend on the project, rate structure, benchmark, performance, speed, quality, and current project rules. The rates shown on this website are examples and should not be treated as a guaranteed income.
What happens after I apply?
You should message the recruiter, complete the registration process, receive the appropriate guidance, complete training, and work through the required exams before production.
What is a pushback?
A pushback means work has been returned for correction or additional review. Use the feedback to understand the issue and improve future submissions.
What should I never share?
Never share your passwords, OTPs, banking passwords, or other sensitive security credentials.
